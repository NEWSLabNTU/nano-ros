`nros doctor` now compares a project's toolchain pin with the `nano-ros` revision in its `west.yml`, and reports an error naming both files and both commits when they disagree. A `git` + `rev` pin is compared with its commit, and a `path` pin with the checkout's `HEAD`. A short sha in `west.yml` matches by prefix. A release pin, or a west revision that is a branch or tag, is reported and not compared. West keeps its own pin; the doctor only reports.
