zenoh-pico moves from 1.8.0 to 1.10.1, the zenoh that Humble's `rmw_zenoh_cpp` 0.1.11 links. The session is now driven by zenoh-pico's background executor (RFC-0110): POSIX, Zephyr, NuttX and FreeRTOS run one executor thread in place of the read and lease tasks, while bare metal and ThreadX drive the executor from `zpico_spin_once`. The lease-task knobs still parse but no longer start a thread; the executor stack is the larger of the read and lease stacks.
