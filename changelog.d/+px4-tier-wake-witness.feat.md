PX4 is a Tier 1 framework (RFC-0112 D3), with the real-time promise measured on PX4 SITL. Each PX4 tier's work item counts its runs and the real-clock arrival→dispatch delay, and the generated module's `status` command prints both per tier. Over a 10 s window the tier runs exactly once per deadline or arrival (30 runs for 10 + 20), with arrival→dispatch at 14–29 µs (phase-521 W8 (e), (f)).
