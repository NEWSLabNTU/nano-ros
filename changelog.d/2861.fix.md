An owned Zephyr west entry (`[image.<id>] entry =` naming a west application) now builds with `nros build` without stating `DEPLOY` or `BOARD`: its `nano_ros_add_executable` deploys where a generated application's entry does (`DEPLOY zephyr`). Its workspace dependencies that are Zephyr modules (`zephyr/module.yml`) now join `ZEPHYR_EXTRA_MODULES` on the west line, by the same module-set computation a generated application uses, so their Kconfig symbols are defined. Before, the configure stopped on an empty board key or "DEPLOY required", and on any such symbol as undefined. The same call in a subdirectory of the application, with no `DEPLOY`, is now refused at configure, naming both remedies, instead of building a host program that fails at link.
