A bringup nested below `src/` (`<ws>/src/test_bringup/<bringup>`) now gets its workspace's root, not `<ws>/src`: its generated launch file and resolved model are looked for under `<ws>/build/nros/`, and the Zephyr module hook runs `nros configure` for it. `nros dev image facts`, asked from a bringup, answers that workspace and the bringup's qualified image, and the hook takes both from it. The root is the nearest ancestor holding `.colcon_workspace` with the bringup under its `src/`, else the nearest `src/` outside a package; a bringup in neither is refused when it has a launch to generate. An owned C/C++ Zephyr west entry with no model is now sized by the census of its program, as the same entry is on the CMake road: the module records the census's entity inventory and sizing descriptor before any reader, and its message bounds are seeded. Before, the census was taken and ignored, and the configure either kept every default or stopped on its sizing-descriptor check.
