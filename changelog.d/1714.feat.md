The contract-violation ring keeps the LATEST `CONFIG_NROS_EXECUTOR_MAX_VIOLATIONS` (default 8) verdicts instead of the first, numbers them and counts what it evicted. A board with no wired console can read every stored violation over SWD from the `NROS_VIOLATION_RECORD` symbol (with `CONFIG_NROS_BOOT_REPORT`; decode with `scripts/read-violation-record.py`), see it in a trace as markers 21-24 (with `CONFIG_NROS_TRACE_CALLBACKS`), or log each one with its number and the counters at the end of its spin (`CONFIG_NROS_VIOLATION_DRAIN_REPORT`).
