A zenoh image on a Zephyr board with the native IP stack keeps its session: the executor's socket receive now times out, because `CONFIG_NROS_RMW_ZENOH` selects `CONFIG_NET_CONTEXT_RCVTIMEO`, without which Zephyr ignores `SO_RCVTIMEO`. Before, the receive blocked until the router spoke, the lease and keep-alive ran about once a minute, and the session re-dialled (issue 2105). A build that turns the option off now fails with an `#error` naming the issue.
