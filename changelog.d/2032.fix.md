A Zephyr image built without `CONFIG_POSIX_API` now defines the whole lock part of the platform ABI: the mutex, recursive-mutex and condvar storage probes, `nros_platform_condvar_signal_from_isr` and `nros_platform_task_attr_init`. Their absence made every Zephyr `native_sim` C++ XRCE image fail to link with 56 duplicate symbols, once the native level runner called one of them. ([#2032](https://github.com/NEWSLabNTU/nano-ros/issues/2032))
