On Zephyr, an XRCE session over UDP now wakes its executor when a datagram arrives instead of the executor blocking inside the transport's receive (RFC-0113 D4, phase-521 W4): the executor parks on the session's socket together with an eventfd its wake object carries, through `zsock_poll`. `CONFIG_NROS_RMW_XRCE` now implies `CONFIG_ZVFS` and `CONFIG_ZVFS_EVENTFD`; with eventfds turned off the session keeps the old wait. Measured on `native_sim`, publish-to-callback latency fell from a 3.2–6.3 ms median to 0.4–1.1 ms.
