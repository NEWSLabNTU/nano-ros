A program that makes its own nodes managed in its `main` can now say how many with `[lifecycle] managed_nodes = N` in `system.toml`. The build then reserves each one's lifecycle services, so on XRCE every node's services register without setting `max_service_servers` by hand. A C++ `rclcpp_lifecycle::LifecycleNode` whose lifecycle services fail to register now reports it through `ok()` and `error_what()` instead of carrying on without them.
