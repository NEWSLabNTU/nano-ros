Two C/C++ images that differ only in their panic ending (`PANIC platform` versus none) no longer share one Corrosion cargo directory. `panic-platform` was applied after the shared cargo dir was keyed, so such images took turns rewriting one generated config header and archive. That left `check-sizes-header-mirrors` failing on the metadata probes after a full `nros sync`, including in the scheduled host-tests lane. The end-of-configure re-key now includes features applied late. Images that apply none keep their key (issue 1858).
