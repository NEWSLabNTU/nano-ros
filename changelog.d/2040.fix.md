`just mem-report` (`scripts/nros-mem-report.py`) files a level image's RAM under nano-ros roles instead of `C++ (global namespace)` and `(C / asm / no path)`: `__nros_levels_backing` and `__nros_tier_backing_<i>` as `[executor storage]`, the spawned levels' stacks and threads as `[level task memory]`, `__nros_scheduler_<n>` as `[level scheduler]`, the contract-monitor tables as `[monitor storage]`, and the Zephyr task-stack pool `nros_thread_stacks` as `[platform task stacks]`. The backings and the scheduler are checked against their sizes-header macros, and the task-stack pool against `CONFIG_NROS_ZEPHYR_TASK_SLOTS` and the stack size in the image's `.config`.
