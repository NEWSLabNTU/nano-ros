A sched task's period is no longer truncated to whole milliseconds on FreeRTOS, ThreadX, NuttX or Zephyr. The boot level and every C sched-task runner declare the period in microseconds as their loop's cadence, so release jitter is judged against the real period (a 30 Hz task against 33,333 µs, not 33,000). Each park is bounded by the executor's entry budget, as on Linux, so an idle level no longer wakes once a period. On Linux the boot level's jitter is now judged against its period rather than its spin budget. On a Zephyr image with a tick finer than 1 ms that parks on sockets, the executor no longer plans parks shorter than the millisecond socket poll can keep.
