RFC-0052 now states what nano-ros derives from a service contract. A client's `call:` is checked at build, as before. A client's `timeout`, `on_timeout`, `on_rejected` and `max_transport`, and a server's `idempotent` and `stale_after`, derive nothing on the image: they describe what the node's own code does, and play_launch checks them. A server honouring `stale_after` needs the request's source timestamp, which XRCE-DDS does not provide.
