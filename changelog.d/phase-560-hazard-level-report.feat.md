The image reports the fallback level a hazard's ladder is on. When a contracted hazard is detected, and whenever the ladder falls to a lower level while the fault holds, the hazard observer emits `hazard-level-runtime`, keyed by the hazard: measured is the level's index in the ladder (0 is the top), declared is the ladder's length, and the level's name appears in the log line (`level=<name>`) and in the `/diagnostics` message. This is the answer play_launch's observer gives as `mode-availability`, so both sides of a bridge name the same level for the same fault. `RungSpec`, `nros_cpp_hazard_rung_t` and `Violation` gain a `level` name (`nros_cpp_violation_t` gains `level` and `level_len`), and `NROS_CPP_HAZARD_RUNG_STORAGE` grows to 48 bytes on a 64-bit host and 32 on a 32-bit target. The violation record and the trace markers keep their layout; the new rule takes wire code 17.
