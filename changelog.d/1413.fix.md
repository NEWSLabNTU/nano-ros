`[tool.play_launch_parser].source.ref` and the `packages/cli/third-party/play_launch` gitlink are two pins on one component, and three documents claimed they "cannot disagree" while nothing measured it — they had drifted ~40 commits (`838ce948` vs `07f0461e`). All three claims are corrected (the index, `just/workspace.just`, and `nano-ros-sdk`'s `build-play_launch_parser.sh`) and replaced by `check-play-launch-parser-ref`, which measures what is actually required: `upstream` and `source.ref` name the same commit, `source.ref` is an ancestor-or-equal of the gitlink (the index may LAG the submodule, never lead it), and while they differ the index carries a `# ref-lag:` reason that must be deleted when they converge. NOT an equality gate: the two are legitimately unequal because issue 0897 moved pyo3 out of the CLI crate, so at the gitlink `.launch.py` hard-errors and — measured, and worse — `$(eval '1 + 1')` exits 0 emitting the substitution UNEXPANDED.
