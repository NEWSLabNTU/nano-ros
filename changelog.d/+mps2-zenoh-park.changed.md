A bare-metal mps2-an385 zenoh image now parks its executor between spins: the board's wake object sleeps on `wfi` until the next deadline or a frame (LAN9118) or byte (UART0) arrives, the zenoh session states `Notify` readiness, and it reports zenoh-pico's real next lease, keep-alive or query timeout instead of the 3.3 s keep-alive interval (RFC-0113 D5/D6). Boards that state their link's receive interrupt ends the park call `nros_rmw_zenoh::set_caller_rx_wakes_park`.
