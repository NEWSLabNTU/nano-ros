A Rust guard condition can now be triggered from an interrupt handler with `GuardCondition::trigger_from_isr()`, which wakes a parked executor through the port's interrupt-safe signal and returns whether it did. `trigger()` must not be called from an interrupt: on FreeRTOS its `xSemaphoreGive` trips the scheduler's assert. FreeRTOS and POSIX (from a signal handler) are supported and tested. Zephyr, ThreadX, NuttX and bare-metal MPS2 refuse for now, and a trigger there is seen at the executor's next deadline (issue 2696).
