Concurrent loggers on a multi-core Zephyr image no longer lose records to the console. The bound that stops a re-entrant sink from recursing through its own logging is now counted per task, through a new platform hook (`nros_platform_current_task_id`), so loggers on different threads no longer use it up: before, more than eight threads logging at once pushed records into a 4-slot queue and about two thirds were lost. Platforms without the hook (the bare-metal boards) keep the process-wide bound.
