The hosted C++ surface has upstream's value-object parameter API: `rclcpp::ParameterValue` and `rclcpp::Parameter` (vectors included), `declare_parameter(name, ParameterValue[, descriptor])` with either nano-ros's descriptor or `rcl_interfaces::msg::ParameterDescriptor`, `get_parameter(name)` / `get_parameters` / `set_parameter(Parameter)` / `set_parameters`, `add_on_set_parameters_callback(f)` over `std::vector<rclcpp::Parameter>` returning an owning handle that unregisters on destruction, and `get_node_parameters_interface()`. All of it forwards to the executor's one parameter store. An on-set callback now sees a proposed bool, integer, double or byte array's elements (`nros_cpp_param_write_t` gained `*_array` + `array_len`), and `set_parameters_atomically` accepts those arrays. `declare_parameter<std::vector<bool>>` now compiles.
