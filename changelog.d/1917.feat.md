`rclcpp::init_in(argc, argv[, session_name])` initializes a session honouring `--ros-args` and returns a refused argument as a `Result` — the counterpart of `rclcpp::init(argc, argv)`, which aborts. `rclcpp::init_with_launch_auto(argc, argv)` and `rclcpp::init_with_launch(path, argc, argv)` are deprecated in its favour: they read the environment and `--ros-args`, never a launch file. Replace either call with `rclcpp::init_in(argc, argv)`; check the path yourself if you relied on `init_with_launch`'s existence check (issue 1917).
