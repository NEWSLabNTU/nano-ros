The LAN9118 lwIP driver no longer loses a received frame's interrupt when the frame lands while the drain task re-arms it: re-arming now checks the RX FIFO afterwards and drains again at once, instead of sleeping the full poll interval while the rest of the burst overflows the part's ~7-frame FIFO. The `mps2-an385-freertos` board now drives RX from the LAN9118 interrupt (IRQ 13) like `mps3-an536-freertos`, and the driver feeds lwIP's link statistics (`LINK_STATS`), including the part's own dropped-frame counter, so loss at the NIC is visible.
