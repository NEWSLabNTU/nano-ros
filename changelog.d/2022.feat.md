A bare-metal mps2-an385 image no longer busy-polls the network while it waits: the zenoh read loop and the smoltcp TCP/UDP wait loops sleep on `wfi` until their deadline, smoltcp's next timer, or an incoming frame (new LAN9118 receive interrupt + SysTick one-shot). Idle host CPU of a QEMU listener drops from 99.9 % to about 1 %. Other bare-metal boards keep the busy-poll until they install a sleep primitive (`nros_smoltcp::set_wait_idle`). ([#2022](https://github.com/NEWSLabNTU/nano-ros/issues/2022))
