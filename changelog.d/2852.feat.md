The `linux-aarch64-musl` board now states its app's stack, so `nros build` checks it instead of reporting it as not checked. The app runs on the process's main thread, and the image holds that thread's stack limit to `memory.app_stack_bytes` (default 256 KiB) at boot: it raises a lower soft limit and logs the old and new limit, and refuses to start, naming the knob, when the hard limit is below it.
