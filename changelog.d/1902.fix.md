Every name a board descriptor publishes is now accepted as an entry board key: `nros codegen entry --typed --board threadx-riscv64` (the qemu-riscv64 ThreadX board's own name) no longer fails with "unknown board key", and neither do the Zephyr `mps2-an385-zephyr` and `qemu-cortex-a53` boards. Three entry keys that no board descriptor names, and that no `nros build` image could therefore select, are removed: `armfvp` (Zephyr's FVP runner; write `fvp-aemv8r-smp`), `freertos-qemu-mps2-an385` (write `mps2-an385-freertos`) and `qemu-rtic-mps2-an385` (write `rtic-mps2-an385`).
