PX4 SITL now runs in CI: a hosted `px4-sitl` job in the nightly workflow builds SITL with the uORB example and runs the PX4 cells. The uORB interop test moved from the out-of-workspace `nros-px4-sitl-test` crate into `nros-tests` (`--test px4_sitl`), so `just px4 test-sitl-example` no longer needs the `px4-rs` harness, and the `(Px4, Cpp, Uorb)` matrix cell is `Gated` on that job instead of carved out (phase-521 W8 (e)).
