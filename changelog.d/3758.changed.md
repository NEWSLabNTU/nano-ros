The build-time FTTI budget charges every level of a hazard's fallback sequence, not only the minimal risk condition (rlm v0.3.18). Each level the hazard runs (the model's `degradation_levels`) has one row: detection, the dwelling levels waited out above it (route plus charged dwell, a dwell range's `max`), the level's route and the settle of its safe state. A level without a dwell is judged against the hazard's `ftti` and its own `within`, a dwelling level against its `within` only, and the binding row is the judged pair with the largest share of its deadline, the same rule play_launch applies. A contract whose level above the floor misses the FTTI, or whose level misses its `within`, is now refused at build time; the message names the binding row and every level's total. `HazardBudget` gains `levels`, `judgements()`, `binding()` and `misses()`; `exceeds_ftti()` is now `misses_a_deadline()`.
