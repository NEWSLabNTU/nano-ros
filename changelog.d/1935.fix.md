`nros setup --tool <name> --source <name>` now installs the tools and then provisions the sources. Before, it installed the tools, never touched the sources, and exited 0. Selector combinations that `nros setup` cannot honour together (a board beside `--tool`/`--source`, `--source` with `--check`, anything beside `--list`/`--licenses`/`--system`, `--rmw` without a board) are now refused with an error naming the flag, instead of being silently ignored.
