On bare-metal MPS2-AN385, on Zephyr, on bare-metal ThreadX and on NuttX, `GuardCondition::trigger_from_isr` now wakes a parked executor (it returned `false` before); on NuttX it also wakes a park on OS handles, which needs `CONFIG_SCHED_LPWORK`. On bare-metal MPS2-AN385 the executor's park also no longer loses a wake signalled from an interrupt between its flag check and its `wfi`. The hosted ThreadX Linux port still returns `false`.
