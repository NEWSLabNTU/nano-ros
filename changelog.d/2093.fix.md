A CycloneDDS or XRCE image no longer prints `ZPICO_TL_RETAIN_BYTES not derived` at configure. The transient-local retention slot is a zenoh-pico knob, so its refusal is now reported only to a zenoh image, or to one that has not stated a backend, and it is named `NROS_ZENOH_TL_RETAIN_BYTES`. The cargo-leaf road no longer names zenoh's `LARGE_PAYLOADS` to a non-zenoh leaf either (issue 2093).
