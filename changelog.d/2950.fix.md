On zenoh, an image whose router address drops connection attempts no longer waits about two minutes per connect: with the default one-attempt budget the open fails within about a second, and with `connect_forever` each attempt gives up after 10 s and retries. An image whose router is across a lossy or high-latency link, where the TCP connect can take over a second, states a budget with `[image.<id>.knobs.zenoh] connect_timeout_ms` and the open retries within it.
