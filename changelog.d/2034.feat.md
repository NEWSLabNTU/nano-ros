The C++ action client's cancel replies are now taken by the executor's spin, as
upstream `rclcpp` delivers them: the spin settles the `async_cancel_*` future
and runs its `cancel_callback` without the future being polled or `poll()`
called. A reply no future claims is kept for `try_recv_cancel_response`
(oldest first, up to `rclcpp_action::CANCEL_STASH_CAP` = 4 per client). Fixed:
a C++ client that cancelled and only spun had its fifth cancel refused, because
an untaken reply held its request's slot.
