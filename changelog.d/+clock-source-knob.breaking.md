The Zephyr options `CONFIG_NROS_SNTP_EPOCH` and `CONFIG_NROS_EPOCH_FROM_ZENOH` are retired, with no alias: a build that sets either stops and names the replacement. State the wall-clock source in the image's `system.toml` instead, as `[image.<id>.knobs.clock] source = "sntp"` (or `"zenoh-hlc"`, or an ordered fallback such as `"sntp,platform"`). `CONFIG_NROS_EPOCH_FROM_ZENOH_INTERVAL_MS` is now `clock.hlc_interval_ms`; `CONFIG_NROS_SNTP_SERVER` and `CONFIG_NROS_SNTP_TIMEOUT_MS` keep their names as `sntp.server` and `sntp.timeout_ms`. The knob registry gains the owner word `clock`, so a downstream package that already uses that word for its own knobs must rename them.
