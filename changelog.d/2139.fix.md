A fixture row can declare the coordinates of the cells that use it as a peer (`serves = ["platform,lang,rmw", ...]` in `examples/fixtures.toml`), and a lane then builds and resolves it whenever one of those cells is selected. The C observer of the RTOS `entry_e2e` pub/sub cells declares its eleven cells, so the tier-2 nightly runs them instead of skipping them as out of lane (issue 2139).
