A nano-ros action server now acts on the zero-id forms of `CancelGoal`, in C, C++ and Rust alike. A zero goal id with a zero stamp cancels every cancelable goal. A zero id with a stamp cancels the goals accepted at or before that stamp. A goal id with a stamp cancels that goal plus those before the stamp. The cancel callback is asked once per selected goal, and the reply lists every goal that moved to CANCELING. A cancel naming an unknown goal now answers `UNKNOWN_GOAL_ID` (or `GOAL_TERMINATED`) without consulting the callback, as rcl_action does. Goals are stamped at acceptance on the ROS clock, and the stamp now appears in the SendGoal reply, the status array and `goals_canceling`. Rust's `ActionServer::try_handle_cancel` takes an `FnMut`, and `RawActiveGoal` gained `stamp`. (issue 2029)
