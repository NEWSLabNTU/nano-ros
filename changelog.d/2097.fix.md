`nros sync` probes a component again after `nros setup --source` provisions the source its probe was missing. Before, the failed probe was cached at its source digest and skipped from then on. The image was then sized without its probe sidecars: on an S32K344 reference image the level backing grew from 33,176 B to 64,592 B and DTCM overflowed, while the build's warning said to run `nros sync`, which could not help.
