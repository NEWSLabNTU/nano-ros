Three entry board keys that no board descriptor names, and that no `nros build` image could therefore select, are removed from `nros codegen entry --board` and `nros::main!`: `armfvp` (Zephyr's FVP runner; write `fvp-aemv8r-smp`), `freertos-qemu-mps2-an385` (write `mps2-an385-freertos`) and `qemu-rtic-mps2-an385` (write `rtic-mps2-an385`).
