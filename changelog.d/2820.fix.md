A CycloneDDS image that serves services (every image does now, through the stock parameter services) no longer overflows a 64 KiB FreeRTOS app task. The Cyclone service paths copied every request and reply through a 64 KiB stack buffer. They now copy straight between the DDS sample and the caller's buffer. A service request or reply larger than 64 KiB is also no longer refused on receive.
