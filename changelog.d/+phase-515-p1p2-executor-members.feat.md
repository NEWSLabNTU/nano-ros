C++ executors gain rclcpp's node and future members: `spin_until_future_complete(future, timeout_ms)`, `spin_all(max_ms)`, `add_node(node)`, `spin_node_some(node)` and `spin_node_once(node, timeout_ms)` on `rclcpp::Executor` and `rclcpp::executors::SingleThreadedExecutor`. `add_node` checks that the node was created on that executor and aborts naming it otherwise; `remove_node` is a compile error, since a node cannot leave the executor whose arena holds its entities.
