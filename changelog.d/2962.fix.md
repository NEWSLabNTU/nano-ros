Rust projects for the Raspberry Pi Pico W on Zephyr (rpi-pico-w-zephyr) compile again. Four nano-ros libraries had chosen how portable-atomic emulates atomics on a chip without compare-and-swap, which clashed with the choice the Zephyr Rust support makes. That choice now belongs to the image: the Zephyr crate makes it for Rust, and `nros-c` for C and C++.
