Cyclone DDS on the host-simulator boards (freertos-posix, threadx-linux) now ends an executor's park when a sample arrives. Cyclone's receive thread is a host thread those kernels do not own, so it may not signal the executor's wake object directly; it now posts a port-owned forwarder (`nros/platform_foreign_wake.h`) that signals it from kernel context. Before this, the session claimed `Notify` while nothing ever posted the wake, and an arrival waited for the next timer. A Cyclone build with neither foreign wake nor the forwarder now states no readiness form and is refused at open (issue 2217).
