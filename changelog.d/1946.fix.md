On the CMake road, a generated C/C++ entry now opens its session on the image's own `domain_id` (`[image.<id>]`, else `[system]`) rather than domain 0. A `-DNROS_DOMAIN_ID` that disagrees with the stated domain stops the configure instead of silently winning.

A standalone CMake leaf's `domain_id` now reaches its session on both CMake roads, and a `-DNROS_DOMAIN_ID` that disagrees with it stops the configure instead of silently winning. `-DNROS_IMAGE=<id>` now selects a leaf's image on every road, not only Zephyr's, so the threadx-linux and rv-virt-threadx leaves state their Cyclone domains in a `[image.<board>-cyclonedds]` image and the fixture lanes no longer pass `-DNROS_DOMAIN_ID` over a leaf that said 0 (issue 1946).
