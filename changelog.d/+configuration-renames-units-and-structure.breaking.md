Forty-one built-in settings are renamed, and one removed, so that every key is `<owner>.<name>` and every memory size is in bytes. The old names are refused, with a message that names the new one and, when the unit changed, the number to multiply by. This applies to every place a setting is written: `system.toml` (`[image.<id>.knobs]`), a board or platform `[knobs]` table, the build environment, a Zephyr conf file or `-DCONFIG_`, and `-DNROS_OVERRIDE_`. The environment variable and the Kconfig symbol follow the key: `zenoh.frag_max_bytes` is `NROS_ZENOH_FRAG_MAX_BYTES` and `CONFIG_NROS_ZENOH_FRAG_MAX_BYTES`. The renames, old key to new key:

- The zenoh sub-tables are gone. `[knobs.zenoh.wire]`, `[knobs.zenoh.limits]` and `[knobs.zenoh.tx]` become plain `[knobs.zenoh]` keys: `wire.batch_multicast_size` to `batch_multicast_bytes`, `wire.batch_unicast_size` to `batch_unicast_bytes`, `wire.frag_max_size` to `frag_max_bytes`, `wire.get_poll_interval_ms` to `get_poll_interval_ms`, `wire.get_reply_buf_size` to `get_reply_buffer_bytes`, `wire.rx_task_budget_frames` to `rx_task_budget_frames`, `wire.rx_task_budget_rest_us` to `rx_task_budget_rest_us`, `limits.keyexpr_string_size` to `keyexpr_string_bytes`, `limits.connect_forever` to `connect_forever`, `limits.connect_timeout_ms` to `connect_timeout_ms`, `limits.subscriber_ring_depth` to `subscriber_ring_depth`, `tx.batch` to `tx_batch`, `tx.flush_ms` to `tx_batch_flush_ms` and `tx.split_lock` to `tx_split_lock`.
- `zenoh.limits.service_timeout_ms` becomes `service.timeout_ms` (`[knobs.service] timeout_ms`; the variable stays `NROS_SERVICE_TIMEOUT_MS`).
- Other zenoh sizes: `zenoh.service_buffer_size` to `zenoh.service_buffer_bytes` (`CONFIG_NROS_SERVICE_BUFFER_SIZE` to `CONFIG_NROS_ZENOH_SERVICE_BUFFER_BYTES`), `zenoh.graph_cache_size` to `zenoh.graph_cache_bytes`, `zenoh.publisher_tx_buffer_size` to `zenoh.publisher_tx_buffer_bytes`, `zenoh.subscriber_large_size` to `zenoh.subscriber_large_bytes`, `zenoh.subscriber_size_threshold` to `zenoh.subscriber_large_threshold_bytes`.
- XRCE: `xrce.buffer_size`, `xrce.builtin_service_request_buffer_size`, `xrce.service_reply_buffer_size`, `xrce.service_request_buffer_size` and `xrce.subscriber_buffer_size` end `_bytes` instead of `_size`.
- Executor: `executor.arena_size` to `executor.arena_bytes`, `executor.subscription_buffer_size` to `executor.subscription_buffer_bytes`, `executor.param_service_buffer_size` to `executor.param_service_buffer_bytes`, and `executor.backing_u64s` to `executor.backing_bytes`, which is now in bytes: multiply the old value by 8 (`NROS_EXECUTOR_BACKING_BYTES`).
- Parameters: `params.max_param_name_len` to `params.max_name_bytes`, `params.max_string_value_len` to `params.max_string_value_bytes`, `params.max_byte_array_len` to `params.max_byte_array_bytes`, `params.max_param_description_len` to `params.max_description_bytes`, `params.max_param_constraints_len` to `params.max_constraints_bytes`. Their variables gain the `PARAMS_` owner (`NROS_MAX_PARAM_NAME_LEN` to `NROS_PARAMS_MAX_NAME_BYTES`, and so on).
- `log.buffer_size` to `log.buffer_bytes`, `subscriber.buffer_size` to `subscriber.buffer_bytes`, and `net.buffer_size` to `net.buffer_bytes` (`NROS_SMOLTCP_BUFFER_SIZE` to `NROS_NET_BUFFER_BYTES`).
- The heap and the application task stack are one setting each on every platform, in bytes: `memory.heap_bytes` (`NROS_MEMORY_HEAP_BYTES`) replaces `zephyr.heap_size` (`NROS_ZEPHYR_HEAP_SIZE`, same value) and `freertos.heap_kb` (`NROS_FREERTOS_HEAP_KB`, multiply by 1024); `memory.app_stack_bytes` (`NROS_MEMORY_APP_STACK_BYTES`) replaces `freertos.app_stack_kb` and `threadx.app_stack_kb` (multiply by 1024). A FreeRTOS heap or app stack must be a whole number of KiB; any other value is refused, naming the nearest valid ones.
- The ThreadX byte pool is `threadx.byte_pool_bytes` (`NROS_THREADX_BYTE_POOL_BYTES`), replacing `NROS_THREADX_BYTE_POOL_KB` (multiply by 1024). When it is not set, the ThreadX pool takes `memory.heap_bytes`, as before; its default, derived from the app stack and the middleware, is unchanged.
- `runtime.let_buffer_size` (`NROS_LET_BUFFER_SIZE`) is removed: it sized nothing. Setting it is refused; delete it.
