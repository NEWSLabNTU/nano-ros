A stock ament_cmake workspace now builds for FreeRTOS and Zephyr with its packages' CMakeLists unchanged. On a board a plain `add_executable` becomes an image, a C++ target's message dependencies are generated, a ported package's C++ is hosted, and plain `target_link_libraries` calls work beside nano-ros's own links. The `add_subdirectory` road also takes the board, locator and domain from `system.toml`. New template: `examples/templates/ament-workspace-port`.
