nano-ros nodes can publish `/rosout`, so `ros2 topic echo /rosout`, `rqt_console` and launch-side log aggregators see them: turn on the `rosout` feature, create an `rcl_interfaces/msg/Log` publisher with `nros::rosout::qos()` (upstream's profile) or `nros::rosout::qos_bounded()` (VOLATILE, and free of the zenoh queryable slot a transient-local publisher costs), call `nros::rosout::enable()` and `nros::rosout::pump()` from your spin loop. The sink deliberately does NOT publish — it copies into a bounded static ring, because a sink that enters the transport on a path that can itself log is unbounded recursion — and the ring costs 5 760 B of `.bss` on the defaults, nothing at all when the feature is off. C and C++ reach none of it yet (issue 1589)
