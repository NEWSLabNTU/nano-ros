A hand-written CMake configure that names its image now gets that image's facts. `nano_ros_workspace(SYSTEM <bringup>)` configured with `-DNROS_IMAGE=<id>` applies the image's `[image.<id>.knobs.*]` and `env` rows and its `domain_id`, and refuses when it names no image while the bringup states such rows. `nano_ros_add_executable(… BRINGUP <dir> IMAGE <id>)` bakes the domain that image states instead of 0, and refuses a `-DNROS_DOMAIN_ID` that names another.
