A zenoh client session open no longer takes 104 s to fail against a peer that accepts the TCP connect and never answers: it now makes one attempt by default and fails after zenoh-pico's 10 s handshake deadline. A board that boots before its router states `[knobs.zenoh.limits] connect_forever = true` (or a budget, `connect_timeout_ms`) to keep retrying until the router answers; zenoh-pico retries transport errors only, with a sleeping backoff. The test-sized retry loop in `nros-rmw-zenoh` is gone.
