An image whose sizing could not be derived is now judged by why: when it declares nothing to derive from, `nros` prints a note under any policy; when the derivation failed, it warns, and refuses under `NROS_PROBE_FAILURE=refuse`. A new optional `[image.<id>] no_inventory = "<reason>"` records why an image declares nothing, in place of the note; it never excuses a failure and is refused on an image that does derive. See the `system.toml` reference. ([details](https://github.com/NEWSLabNTU/nano-ros/pull/4304))
