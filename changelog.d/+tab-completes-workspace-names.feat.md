`<Tab>` now completes a workspace's own names too: images for `nros build`, `run` and `flash`, packages for the `--packages-*` selections, the workspace's RMW providers, and its extension verbs. `nros configure` and `nros build` record them in `build/nros/complete.json`; until the first of those runs, completion offers only the SDK's names (boards, RMWs, knobs). `--board` and `--rmw` complete on `nros setup`, `new`, `build`, `doctor` and `plan`.
