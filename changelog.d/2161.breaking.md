Retired the never-used per-node dispatch-slot registry: `nros::node!` no longer emits `register_dispatch(executor)` or the `#[no_mangle] __nros_node_<pkg>_on_callback` trampoline, `Executor::register_dispatch_slot` / `dispatch_slot_count` / `dispatch_callback` and `nros::__macro::__private_node_state_into_raw` are gone, and the generated `run_plan.rs` no longer carries `run_plan_register_dispatch`. All were `#[doc(hidden)]` and nothing called them; RTIC registers through `<pkg>::register` and runs callbacks inline. Deferred dispatch is `Executor::set_deferred` (issue 2146). Each executor backing is one slot table smaller (issue 2161).
