The rest of upstream `rclcpp_action`'s goal-handle API on a hosted C++ build:
`Client<A>::async_get_result(goal_handle, result_callback)`,
`async_cancel_goal(goal_handle, cancel_callback)`, `async_cancel_all_goals` and
`async_cancel_goals_before(stamp)` return upstream's `std::shared_future`s
(`WrappedResult`, `CancelResponse::SharedPtr` with `return_code` and
`goals_canceling`), which `rclcpp::spin_until_future_complete` then `get()`
complete; the future drives the client while it waits. `ServerGoalHandle::execute()`
(and `Server<A>::execute(goal_id)`) move an `ACCEPT_AND_DEFER` goal to EXECUTING
and publish the status, and `create_server` now executes an `ACCEPT_AND_EXECUTE`
goal before `handle_accepted`, as upstream does.
Fixed: a C++ action future dropped before its reply kept one of the client's
four in-flight slots forever, so after four abandoned goals (or get_result
requests) no more could be sent. The slot is freed when the future is
abandoned, unless a callback still waits on that reply, and a late reply is
dropped.
