A hazard's rung route is monitored on the image. play_launch now writes each rung of a hazard's ladder into the SystemModel with the route its checker charged (from the detection to the rung's first output), and the hazard observer judges the top rung the executor can see: from the tick that reported `hazard-detected` to the first publish on that rung's output. A rung that answers late, or not at all within its route, is reported once per detection as `hazard-route-runtime`, on the Rust `nros::main!` road and both C/C++ entry shapes. The reaction hook's event gains `fired_at_us`, the executor's monotonic microseconds when the verdict fired, so a hook can time its own reaction from the detection. `NROS_CPP_HAZARD_ROW_STORAGE` grows to 96 bytes on a 64-bit host and 64 on a 32-bit target.
