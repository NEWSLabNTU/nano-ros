FreeRTOS on the Cortex-R52 boards (`mps3-an536-freertos`, `s32z270-freertos`) now gives every task an FPU context (`configUSE_TASK_FPU_SUPPORT 2`, `configFPU_D32=1`). Without it, hard-float tasks clobbered each other's VFP/NEON registers, and a CycloneDDS participant went silent about 100 ms after discovery while its app kept publishing.
