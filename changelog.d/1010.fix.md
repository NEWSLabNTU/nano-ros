Every Zephyr XRCE example boots and delivers again. `xrce_session_state_t` is one heap allocation out of the platform arena, and at the defaults the twelve C and Rust overlays carried it was 423,824 bytes against 65,536 — 62.9 % entity slots the image does not have, and 30.9 % reliable stream buffer sized at an MTU no conf could reach. The twelve overlays now state their entity caps the way the six C++ ones have since issue 1033, and `CONFIG_NROS_XRCE_CUSTOM_TRANSPORT_MTU` (defaulting to `CONFIG_NROS_XRCE_TRANSPORT_MTU`) makes the MTU of the only transport a Zephyr image links settable for the first time — the general knob binds `UCLIENT_UDP/TCP_TRANSPORT_MTU`, both of which sit behind a profile no Zephyr build defines.
