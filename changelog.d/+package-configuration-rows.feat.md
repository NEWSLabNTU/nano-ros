RMW, platform and board packages declare their own configuration rows in an `nros-knobs.toml` beside their descriptor; the zenoh, XRCE-DDS and Cyclone DDS backends and the FreeRTOS, ThreadX and Zephyr platforms now do. A package's rows sit under its owner word, which is its RMW or platform name (or, for a board, one of its board keys): `nros configure` refuses a row under another owner's word, two packages claiming one word, and a name two rows use, and reports a package found in more than one search root instead of refusing it. No knob, variable or Kconfig symbol changed name.
