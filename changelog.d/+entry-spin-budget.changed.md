An idle entry no longer wakes every 10 ms: `nros::main!`, the board entries, the C and C++ entries and `rclcpp::spin` ask the executor for their spin budget (`Executor::entry_spin_budget`, `nros_cpp_entry_spin_budget_ms`), which is up to a minute when every session can end the park itself and no component polls from `tick`, else the 10 ms it was. A component that overrides `tick` keeps the 10 ms cadence unless it sets `ExecutableNode::HAS_TICK = false`. A cancel and a SIGTERM to a hosted image now end a park at once.
