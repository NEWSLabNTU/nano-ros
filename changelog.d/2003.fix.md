`zpico-alloc` (the board heap behind `z_malloc` and the Zephyr C funnel) now
returns blocks aligned to `2 * size_of::<usize>()`: 16 bytes on 64-bit
targets, as a C `malloc` owes there. Its small-allocation slab handed out
8-aligned blocks whenever the linker placed the heap at 8 mod 16, and Cyclone
DDS's lock-free list then faulted on AArch64 (`caspal`) at boot. No heap sizing
changes: 16 is already the TLSF payload alignment on 64-bit.
