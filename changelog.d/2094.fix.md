A NuttX zenoh node with no traffic of its own (a listener nobody publishes to, an idle service server) no longer loses its session every 60 s. NuttX linked a zenoh-pico clock with the wrong calling convention, so the session's keep-alive and lease were scheduled at garbage times; the correct clock (`nuttx_clock.c`) is now the one linked, and it counts nanoseconds in 64 bits (issue 2094).
