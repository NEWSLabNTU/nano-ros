A Rust component can read the metadata of the message its subscription callback is handling: declare the subscription with `create_subscription_for_callback_name_with_message_info` and call `ctx.message_info()` in `on_callback` for the source and received timestamps, the publisher GID and the sequence number. It is opt-in because the metadata is staged beside the payload, so such a subscription cannot dispatch in place and the build reserves its receive buffer. Plain subscriptions are unchanged (issue 2349).
