`nros-sdk-index.toml` no longer lists rosidl's own Python dependencies as ours. `empy` and `lark` now sit under `[source.rosidl.python]`, keyed by upstream's rosdep keys, and take their apt package names from the pinned rosdep snapshot. The empy 3.3.4 pin is kept, as our constraint. `check-python-entry-provenance` compares that table with rosidl's `package.xml` at the pinned commit. What `nros setup --check` and the runner image install is unchanged (issue 1483).
