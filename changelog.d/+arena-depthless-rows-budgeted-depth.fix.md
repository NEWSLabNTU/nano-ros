The executor arena model no longer drops to the image-wide receive class when a subscription row of the sizing descriptor states no QoS depth. Such a row is now priced at the image's budgeted depth (`NROS_PUBSUB_QOS_DEPTH`, the depth the fallback charged every subscription anyway) and at its own type's slot, so a stated `NROS_PUBSUB_QOS_DEPTH=1` takes effect per row. On an image with one large subscribed type and a large stated `NROS_SUBSCRIPTION_BUFFER_SIZE`, the derived arena was many times the claim and the arena oracle refused any smaller stated value: seven KEEP_LAST(1) subscriptions with a 64 KiB closure buffer were modelled at 1,385,536 B against 90,552 B claimed at first spin.
