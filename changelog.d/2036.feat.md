C++ (hosted): a subscription callback may take upstream's `const rclcpp::MessageInfo&` as its second argument, after the message as `const M&`, `std::shared_ptr<const M>`, `std::shared_ptr<M>` or `std::unique_ptr<M>`. `MessageInfo::get_rmw_message_info()` returns the source and received timestamps, both sequence numbers and the publisher GID the backend reported for that sample, all zero where it reports none. `SubscriptionOptions::message_info = true` with a callback that takes no `MessageInfo` now aborts the create instead of being ignored. A freestanding build refuses the shape at compile time.
