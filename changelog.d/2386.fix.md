`nros build` of a C/C++ cross image now works on a workspace that has never been built. Before, its entity census, taken from the matching native image, failed with "has not been configured", and the cross image then stopped on "census missing". The native image's configure stamp is now recorded before the census builds it. On a shared CMake configure, the census of every image that configure checks is now taken, not only the image you asked for.
