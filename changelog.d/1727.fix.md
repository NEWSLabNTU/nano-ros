The contract monitors' `/diagnostics` reporter no longer publishes inside the spin that judged the violation: verdicts are queued and sent at the end of a spin whose next timer leaves room for the report, several coalesced into one `DiagnosticArray`, and the reporter's own time is not counted as release jitter. On a slow serial link a single verdict used to cost enough spin time to trip `release-jitter-runtime` and `timer-overrun-runtime` verdicts of its own.
