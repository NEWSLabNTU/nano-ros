nano-ros ships a Zephyr board for the NXP S32Z270 RTU0 Cortex-R52, `s32z270-rtu0-zephyr` (`s32z2xxdc2@D/s32z270/rtu0`, build and link only: no emulator models it). A board descriptor's `west_board` may now carry a board revision (`board@rev/soc/variant`): the `BOARD_<X>` Kconfig symbol and the per-board file names drop the revision the way Zephyr does, so no `-DBOARD_REVISION` is needed (issue 2631).
