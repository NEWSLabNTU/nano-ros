ros-launch-manifest v0.3.10 and play_launch 0.27.0 (phase 536 W1): a scheduling tier is a task. A `system.toml` writes `[tasks.<name>]` (and `[tasks.<name>.<target>]`) and `[[component]] group_tasks`; every tracked bringup is migrated. The old `[tiers.*]` and `group_tiers` still read this release, with one `deprecated-spelling` warning per key from the resolver, and are refused by the next rlm release; `play_launch migrate <system.toml> --write` rewrites a file. nano-ros's own C, C++ and Rust names, Kconfig symbols and generated symbols keep the word until phase 536 W2.
