A CMake configure that builds an entry of a different bringup than the one `nano_ros_workspace(SYSTEM …)` names is now refused when it lacks the capability axes (`[system] features`) that entry's own bringup declares. The error names both bringups and the missing axes. Before, the entry compiled without them and nothing was reported. ([details](https://github.com/NEWSLabNTU/nano-ros/pull/4243))
