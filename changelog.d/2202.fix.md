`just mem-report` now counts weak and unique objects (`nm` types `V`, `v`, `u`) by the section they live in. Before, a C++ image's executor storage (`rclcpp::Node::GlobalStorageHolder<0>::storage`, a template's static data member) was in no RAM total. It now appears under `[executor storage]` and in the top-RAM list, instead of in the unattributed gap.
