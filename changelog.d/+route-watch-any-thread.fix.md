A port that supervises a route with its own timer (`mechanism: timer`) can now read the route from outside the spin thread: `Executor::route_watch` / `nros::monitor::RouteWatch` and, in C, `nros_cpp_executor_route_watch` + `nros_cpp_route_watch_next_due_us` read the row's atomic state at the moment of the call, from any thread or interrupt. A route answered before its due by a callback of a spin that then ran past the due used to look like a miss at the timer's expiry, because the port learned the answer only from `nros_cpp_executor_route_next_due_us` after the spin returned (a false safe state on a board); the watch reads it as answered, and a real miss as due at the same instant (phase-503 W7).
