Contract monitors can wait for the application to finish start-up: with `CONFIG_NROS_MONITOR_ARM_ON_CALL` they arm when the application calls `nros_monitors_arm()` / `nros::arm_monitors()` / `nros::monitor::request_monitor_arming()` (for example on entering RUN), with `CONFIG_NROS_MONITOR_ARM_GRACE_MS` as a deadline after the first spin. Verdicts before arming are counted (`suppressed_before_arm`) but not stored. The default is unchanged: armed at the first spin.
