The executor's C/C++ entry points left the Rust user API. 63 `Executor` methods that exist for `nros-c` and `nros-cpp` now sit behind a sealed trait, `nros_node::executor::ffi::ExecutorFfi`, which `nros` does not re-export. They are the C-shaped ones (raw pointers, `extern "C"` callbacks, the `unsafe` `release_*` / `detach_*` paths, `from_session_ptr*`, `add_arena_subscription_c_*`, the `*_raw` service and action registration) and the plumbing the shims reach into (`enter_spin_loop`, `exec_lock`, `node_session_mut`, `set_entry_owner`, `timer_elapsed_us` and others). They are the pre- and post-shutdown hook pair too. Code that called one through `nros` should use the Rust-shaped API. A board or shim that needs the FFI form imports the trait from `nros-node`.
