Rust (declarative): a declared timer's callback reads the activation's call info with `CallbackCtx::timer_info()` (`expected_call_time`, `actual_call_time`, on the timer's clock), and `DeclarativeNode::create_timer_for_callback_name_with_autostart(name, period, clock, autostart)` declares a timer stopped. `nros::TimerCallInfo` is re-exported. Every declared timer now registers through `Executor::register_timer_with_info`, the same call as the imperative `Node::create_timer_with_info`.
