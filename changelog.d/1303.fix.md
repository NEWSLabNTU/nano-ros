A failed `rclcpp::Node::create_*` and a refused `--ros-args` token now say why on an RTOS console. Each refusal used to be one log record of 870–1180 bytes, which `nros_log` dropped for not fitting its buffer, so the process aborted after printing only an ellipsis. Each is now two short records: the call and its value, then the reason. The fixed texts behind `NROS_RCLCPP_REFUSE_INIT_ARGV` and `NROS_RCLCPP_ABORT_FAILED_CREATE` are shorter to match.
