On Zephyr, callback trace events and application trace markers now reach the CTF stream with no patch of your own. `nros setup board` adds the `app_marker` CTF event (fields `marker_id` and `arg`) to the Zephyr workspace, which the trace sink forwards to; before, an image with `CONFIG_TRACING_CTF` on recorded no marker and printed no warning. On the 3.7 line it also makes `k_thread_priority_set` reach the CTF stream, which stock 3.7 drops.
