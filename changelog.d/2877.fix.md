`nros build` and `nros run` now take the Zephyr tree of the west workspace a project sits in. A project that is its own west manifest authority (a `.west/` at or above its workspace whose manifest names a `zephyr` project) used to fall through to the store's workspace, `~/.nros/workspaces/zephyr/<line>`, which carries nano-ros's pins and patches rather than the project's, unless every call passed `--zephyr-workspace`. The ladder is now `--zephyr-workspace`, `$ZEPHYR_BASE`, `$NROS_ZEPHYR_WORKSPACE`, the project's west workspace, then the store's, and the build prints one line naming the tree when the project's workspace decided it. A `.west/` whose manifest names no `zephyr`, or whose Zephyr is not checked out yet, is skipped as before.
