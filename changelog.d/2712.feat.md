C programs can read a received message's metadata. `rclc_executor_add_subscription_with_message_info` registers a subscription whose callback gets a `const rmw_message_info_t *`: the source and received timestamps, both sequence numbers and the publisher GID, or NULL when the backend reports none. `rclc_executor_add_subscription_with_info` and `rclc_executor_add_subscription_raw_with_info` are deprecated: despite their names, their callback receives the wire attachment and no metadata. Define `NROS_NO_DEPRECATION_WARNINGS` to silence the warning (issue 2712).
