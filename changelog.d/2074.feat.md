C++: `rclcpp::PollSubscription<M>` gains upstream's polling take with metadata, `take(M& msg, rclcpp::MessageInfo& info)` (plus `take_sized<Cap>(msg, info)`). It reads the message and the sample's timestamps, sequence numbers and publisher GID from one backend take, and it is available on freestanding builds as well as hosted ones. `rclcpp::MessageInfo::is_reported()`, a nano-ros extension, tells a backend that reported no metadata for a sample (XRCE, uORB: every field zero) apart from one that reported zeros. The callback-side `MessageInfo` carries the same flag (issue 2074).
