A spawned level's worker frame is now measured, not estimated, on FreeRTOS and NuttX (issue 2019). A level runs its own setup on its own stack, and that setup is the stack's peak, which the old 4,096 B estimate did not cover. The C headers' defaults are now `NROS_FREERTOS_LEVEL_WORKER_FRAME` 10,240 B and `NROS_NUTTX_LEVEL_WORKER_FRAME` 11,264 B (the per-level defaults are unchanged), and the Rust pack derives over the Rust runner's own frames (FreeRTOS 48,128 B, NuttX 32,768 B): `nros_orchestration_ir::level_worker_frame_bytes` takes a `LevelRunner`. Zephyr and ThreadX keep the 4,096 B estimate; a Zephyr board measures its own and sets `CONFIG_NROS_ZEPHYR_LEVEL_WORKER_FRAME`.
