C++: `rclcpp::Clock::sleep_until(until[, context])` and `sleep_for(rel_time[, context])`. The wait drives the executor -- callbacks run while it blocks, so a single-threaded image keeps its transport -- and a ROS-time clock driven by `/clock` wakes when simulated time arrives. Returns `false` without sleeping before `rclcpp::init`, for a deadline on another clock type, or from inside a callback (phase-515 P5).
