A CAN frame driver ABI, `<nros/platform_can.h>`: `open`, `send` (with an FD flag), `fd_active`, `recv` and `close` over caller-owned handle storage, with Rust bindings, a `Can` wrapper in `nros-platform-cffi`, the `PlatformCan` trait and `nros_platform_export_can!`. The host-only `nros-can-mock` backend records sent frames and replays queued ones, so an application's CAN encoder can be tested on the host with no interface.
