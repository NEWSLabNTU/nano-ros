An image now declares where its wall clock comes from, in `[knobs.clock]`: `source` names one source (`platform`, `sntp`, `zenoh-hlc`) or an ordered fallback, tried at boot before anything is stamped, each for up to `fallback_settle_ms`, and the first that answers is kept for the run. `upgrade = "monotonic"` lets a source earlier in the list take over later, counted in the wall-clock state and never letting a reading go backwards; the default, `"never"`, keeps one clock for the whole run. When no source answers, `epoch_failure = "unstamped"` (the default) says so once and publishes unset stamps, and `"halt"` stops the image with a message naming the sources. A port that cannot resolve a named source refuses the build, naming the port. Knob values that are not one of a knob's stated words are refused at configure on every road.
