`nros platform list` and `nros platform show <platform>` list the platforms boards build on, each with the `nros new --platform` word that scaffolds on it, its boards, the RMWs verified there and its capabilities; a platform added on `$NROS_PLATFORMS_DIR` is listed too. `nros runner list` and `nros runner show <name>` list the runners `nros run`, `nros flash` and `nros monitor` can drive and the extension commands the workspace reaches, with the `system.toml` line that selects each runner. All four take `--json`.
