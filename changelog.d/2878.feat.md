A Zephyr image can select a tracing backend it registers itself: `nros setup board` adds `CONFIG_TRACING_BACKEND_CUSTOM` and `CONFIG_TRACING_BACKEND_CUSTOM_NAME` to the Zephyr 3.7 and 4.4 workspaces, so the CTF stream reaches a backend defined with `TRACING_BACKEND_DEFINE()` outside the Zephyr tree, such as an overwrite-oldest RAM ring, with no Zephyr patch of your own.
