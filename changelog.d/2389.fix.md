A zenoh session open now ends within its connect budget (`[knobs.zenoh.limits] connect_timeout_ms`) even when the peer accepts the TCP connect and never answers: the budget bounds each attempt's handshake read, not only the gap between attempts. Before, one such attempt ran zenoh-pico's own 10 s handshake deadline whatever the budget said (measured on Zephyr native_sim: 10.1 s under a 3 s budget, now 3.03 s). A failed open also prints `zenoh open: no session with <locator> within connect_timeout_ms=<N>`, so the console names the budget it gave up under (issue 2389).
