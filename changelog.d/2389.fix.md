A zenoh client's connect budget (`[knobs.zenoh.limits] connect_timeout_ms`) now bounds each attempt's handshake as well as the retries between attempts. Before, a peer that accepts the TCP connect and never answers held one attempt for zenoh-pico's own 10 s handshake deadline whatever the budget said (measured on Zephyr native_sim with a 3 s budget: 10.1 s, now 3.03 s). A failed open also prints `zenoh open: no session with <locator> within connect_timeout_ms=<N>`, naming the budget it gave up under. The TCP connect itself is still bounded by the network stack, not the budget, on some platforms (issue 2437); the 80 s silence issue 2389 was filed for is not explained by this and stays open.
