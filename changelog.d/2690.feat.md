Variants of one host may share its address. A top-level `[image_alternatives] <group> = ["img-a", "img-b", ...]` in `system.toml` declares images that never run together (one controller built plain, with statistics, with a trace); two images of one group may state the same static address and MAC on a network, so each can name one board network file. Any other two images on one address are still refused, and the refusal now names the table. A group needs two or more declared images, and an image belongs to at most one group.
