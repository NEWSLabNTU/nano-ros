An image whose wall clock comes from the board (`[knobs.clock] source = "board"`) can now ask the board again on an interval with `clock.board_resync_interval_ms` (default 0, asked once as before), so its stamps follow a board clock that drifts or that the board's own time service corrects. The re-ask runs on its own task, whose stack is `clock.board_resync_stack_bytes` (default 8 KiB, raised to the platform's minimum where that is larger); state what your board's clock hook needs. Each answer is applied under `clock.upgrade`, like the first one. Supported on POSIX and FreeRTOS.
