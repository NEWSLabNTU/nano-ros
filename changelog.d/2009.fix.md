Service clients now match each reply to its request by sequence number. A C++
`rclcpp::Future` is keyed by its request's id, so several requests may be in
flight on one `PollClient` and each receives its own reply; a reply that
arrives after `remove_pending_request`, after the future is destroyed, or after
the new `prune_pending_requests()` is dropped instead of being taken by the
client's next future. The same rule applies to the Rust `Promise` (a call
abandoned with `reset_in_flight`), the executor's callback clients, C's
`rclc_client_take_response` / `rclc_client_call` (a timed-out call's late reply
no longer answers the next call), the blocking `call_polling`, and the action
client, whose goal acceptances and results are now attributed to the goal their
request named rather than to the most recently sent one. C polled clients gain
`rclc_polling_client_send_request_raw_seq` / `_take_response_raw_seq`, and
`RawServiceClient::send_request_raw` / `take_response_raw` return the id. The
RMW ABI gains `NROS_RMW_SEQUENCE_ID_UNKNOWN` for backends that cannot assign
ids; such a client keeps one request in flight.
