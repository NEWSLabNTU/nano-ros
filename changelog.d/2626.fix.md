A hand-written image's executor-level lifecycle registration (`rclc_executor_register_lifecycle_services`, `nros_cpp_register_lifecycle_services` without a bound node, `Executor::register_lifecycle_services` / `register_lifecycle_node`) now serves its five REP-2002 services under the image's primary node, so `ros2 lifecycle get /<node>` finds them. A C program's services were `//get_state`, and a Rust or C++ image's sat under the executor's configured name beside its declared nodes. An image that declares no node keeps its configured name. One with no name at all is now refused (`LifecycleNodeUnnamed`) instead of serving `//get_state`. A second lifecycle registration on the primary node is refused.
