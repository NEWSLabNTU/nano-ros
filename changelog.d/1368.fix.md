Every build that compiles zenoh-pico through cargo (FreeRTOS, NuttX, ThreadX, bare-metal, posix) now refuses an image whose stated receive payload class is larger than `max(NROS_ZENOH_FRAG_MAX_SIZE, NROS_ZENOH_BATCH_UNICAST_SIZE)`. Before, such a sample was dropped inside the transport with no error. Zephyr already refused it at configure (issue 1368).
