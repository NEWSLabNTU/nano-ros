A Zephyr zenoh image that is built to receive more than zenoh-pico can reassemble now fails the configure instead of dropping those samples at run time. `NROS_SUBSCRIBER_BUFFER_SIZE` and `ZPICO_SUBSCRIBER_LARGE_SIZE` have been derived from the image's message bounds since phase-403, while `NROS_FRAG_MAX_SIZE` stayed a flat Kconfig `default 2048` that nothing compared them to -- and a fragmented sample that reassembles past it is dropped inside the transport on a success return, with the `_Z_INFO` beside the drop compiled out, so it reaches no callback and raises no error and the subscription simply looks like one that never fires. The build now names both numbers and the knob to raise. The ceiling it checks against is the larger of `ZPICO_FRAG_MAX_SIZE` and `ZPICO_BATCH_UNICAST_SIZE`, because a sample that fits one negotiated batch arrives whole and is never defragmented; on the embedded defaults it is the 2048-byte frag ceiling that binds. `NROS_FRAG_MAX_SIZE` is deliberately still not derived: fragmentation is a property of the transport and the link rather than of the message, and its useful upper end is the path MTU, so the build states the disagreement and leaves the value to whoever owns the board's RAM budget.
