A typed Zephyr entry's baked boot config (RFC-0045, the `.nros_boot_config` section) is now placed in rodata by the nano-ros Zephyr module. It was an orphan section, and on `mr_canhubk3/s32k344` the linker put it in the 0x100-byte IVT_HEADER region, so every typed C/C++ or Rust image for that board failed to link with "region `IVT_HEADER' overflowed by 268 bytes". No application needs a linker fragment of its own any more (issue 2077).
