A contract mode now moves the deadlines of the tiers it derives, and its switch
is a contracted transition. Each mode's table carries the SchedContext
deadlines it changes (the base's restored when the base is entered again), on
the Rust `nros::main!` road and both C/C++ entry shapes. A mode whose switch
cannot fit its reaction path's `max_latency` at the slowest tier's spin is
refused at bake time, and the executor reports `mode-switch-runtime` when
entering a mode waited longer than that budget.
