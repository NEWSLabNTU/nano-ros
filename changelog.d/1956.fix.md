A zenoh subscription that a tier in the same image publishes to, and that
also receives remote samples, no longer wedges. The in-image publish delivers
on the publisher's task, so the subscription's receive ring had two producers
racing on its tail. Under load one could store a stale tail, which left the
ring looking non-empty forever while every new sample was dropped, and the
executor re-ran the same few samples in a loop. The ring's producers now
serialize. Two defects that made this worse are fixed too: an in-place
subscription dispatch takes at most 64 samples per pass instead of draining
until empty, so a refilled ring can no longer hold the executor; and a failed
subscriber or service creation can no longer hand a live buffer slot to the
next creation (issue 1956).
