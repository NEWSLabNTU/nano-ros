An XRCE publish, service request or service reply larger than an XRCE message can state (65,531 bytes of serialized data, 65,507 for a reply) is now refused with a "message too large" error. Before, it reported success, and the sample was lost or delivered cut short. Publish errors now reach the caller as the transport reported them, in Rust, C and C++, instead of as a generic publish failure.
