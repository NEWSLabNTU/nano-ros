An XRCE custom transport can now report arrivals: `nros_rmw_xrce_set_custom_transport_rx_notify` (Rust: `nros_rmw_xrce_cffi::set_custom_transport_rx_notify`) registers a hook the backend hands the executor's wake callback, and a session on that transport states `Notify` readiness, so the executor parks until data arrives instead of blocking in `read`. mps2-an385's UART0 transport registers one from its RX interrupt (RFC-0113 D4).
