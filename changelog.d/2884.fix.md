A Zephyr image with `clock.sntp_resync_interval_ms` above 0 now paces its SNTP re-sync on the server's clock rather than on the image's own. A simulator whose guest clock races (an Arm FVP fast-forwards an idle guest) re-queried the server several times a real second; it now re-queries at the stated interval in real time, from the second re-sync on. The first re-sync after boot, and any gap after the server's time stepped, are still paced on the image's own clock, and the image says when that is the case.
