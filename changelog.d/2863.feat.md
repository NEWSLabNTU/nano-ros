`nros run`, `nros flash --monitor` and `nros monitor` take `--forbid TEXT` and `--forbid-regex REGEX`, which fail the run (exit 1) on the first console line that matches, before or after the expectation is met, and `--settle SECONDS`, which keeps watching for that long after the expectation is met so a later fatal or the rest of a report is still checked (`--timeout` bounds the time to the match only). `nros run` and `nros flash` take `--zephyr-workspace` for the build they start when an image is stale, as `nros build` does.
