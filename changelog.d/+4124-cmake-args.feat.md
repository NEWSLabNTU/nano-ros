`nros build --cmake-args=<args>` passes arguments to every CMake configure of the build: a CMake image's, a west image's and a CMake package's. The `=` is required. One value can hold several arguments, split with plain shell quoting (`--cmake-args="-DA=1 -DB='x y'"`), and the flag repeats. `--cmake-args=@<file>` reads one argument per line, with `#` comments. Allowed: `-D`, `-U`, `-W*`, `--log-level`, `--log-context` and `--debug-find`. A name `nros build` sets itself, and `-DCONFIG_*` (which goes in the image's `conf`), are refused, and the error says where the setting goes. colcon's space form, `--cmake-args -DA=1`, is refused with your arguments shown rewritten. An image states its own in `[image.<id>.build] cmake_args = [...]` in `system.toml`, checked the same way; they follow `[image_defaults.build] cmake_args`, and `--cmake-args=` comes after both.
