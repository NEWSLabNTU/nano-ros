`RCLCPP_<LEVEL>_THROTTLE(logger, clock, ms, ...)` now compiles and throttles, measuring its window on the `clock` you pass as rclcpp does: `*get_clock()`, a steady clock, a system clock or a ROS-time clock driven by `/clock`. The first record at a call site logs, then at most one per `ms`. An invalid clock is reported once per site, and the site is then throttled on the platform's monotonic clock, so a hot loop does not flood the console. `RCLCPP_<LEVEL>_ONCE`, `_SKIPFIRST` and `_SKIPFIRST_THROTTLE` are new, at every level. All four keep their state per call site in function-local statics, with no heap, table or lock, so they work on freestanding targets. The C throttle gains `rclc_log_throttle_admit_at`, which takes the clock reading from the caller (issue 2636, GitHub #4010).
