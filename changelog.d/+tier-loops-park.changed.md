The native C++ multi-tier runner's tier loops and `rclc_executor_spin_period`'s gap between invocations now park on the executor's wake object instead of sleeping. An arrival, guard condition or timer due during the wait is dispatched when it happens rather than at the next period, a cancel ends the wait, and an idle tier no longer wakes at its `spin_period_us` for nothing (RFC-0113 D2).
