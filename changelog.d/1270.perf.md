Parameter services no longer cost 48 KiB of buffers per node: every node's six servers share one request/reply buffer pair per executor (8 KiB at the default `NROS_PARAM_SERVICE_BUFFER_SIZE`). The derived queryable pool (`NROS_MAX_QUERYABLES`, `NROS_XRCE_MAX_SERVICE_SERVERS`) now counts six servers per node when the bringup declares `param_services`, and five when it declares `lifecycle`.
