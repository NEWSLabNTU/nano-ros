`Executor::set_deferred`, `set_defer_notifier` and `run_deferred` / `run_deferred_handle` let a hand-written host defer callbacks: the spin queues a ready deferred callback and calls a plain `fn(ctx, HandleId)` notifier, and the host runs it later from its own task. Before this, only the `nros::node!`-emitted callback ABI could defer dispatch (issue 2146).
