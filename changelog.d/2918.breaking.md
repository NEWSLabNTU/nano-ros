Two bare-metal build settings are renamed onto the knob registry, and the old names now stop the build naming the new one. `NROS_HEAP_SIZE` (the static heap on mps2-an385 and stm32f4) is `NROS_MEMORY_HEAP_BYTES`, the `memory.heap_bytes` knob. `NROS_BAREMETAL_STACK_KB` (the mps2-an385 main stack, in KiB) is `NROS_MEMORY_APP_STACK_BYTES`, the `memory.app_stack_bytes` knob, in bytes and a whole number of KiB: `NROS_BAREMETAL_STACK_KB=56` becomes `NROS_MEMORY_APP_STACK_BYTES=57344`.
