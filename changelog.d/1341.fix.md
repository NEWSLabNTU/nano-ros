The two zenoh action interop cells met a real ROS 2 peer for the first time, and two findings came out of it. The nano-ros side of `native-action-rust-zenoh-n2r` works: the action client drives a stock `examples_rclcpp_minimal_action_server` over `rmw_zenoh_cpp` to Fibonacci(10). The other direction does not run at all — a zenoh action SERVER now fails to start, because its `/fibonacci/_action/status` publisher asks for the TRANSIENT_LOCAL durability `rcl_action_qos_profile_status_default` requires and the shim began refusing that on 2026-09-12 (issue 1341, with the 2026-09-08 build of the same fixture measured still running). Separately, a fixed-domain zenoh cell inherits whatever ros2cli daemon domain 0 already holds, which makes the `ros2 action list` discovery gate unmeetable for a reason that is not about nano-ros (issue 1342).
