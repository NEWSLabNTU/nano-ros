A level image's executor arena is sized by the same model as `NROS_EXECUTOR_ARENA_SIZE`, and now matches it. The one executor that runs every level used to pay the per-executor base overhead once per level. Subscription rows from the sizing descriptor are used whenever every subscription declares its QoS, even when a service endpoint declares none. Before, such an image fell back to a path that reserved a receive region for `in_place` subscriptions, which never claim one. The size probe behind `NROS_CPP_LEVELS_BACKING_SIZE` and the tier backings now builds `nros-rmw` with the dispatch features the image links. On the S32K344 safety island the level backing goes from 40,640 B to 33,176 B of DTCM. The arena goes from 29,820 B to 22,352 B, and the image uses 11,976 B of it.
