On the FreeRTOS MPS2-AN385 board, a task that overflows its stack now stops with `*** STACK OVERFLOW: <task> ***`, naming the task. Before, the overflow wrote over the heap block below the stack, and the image reported `*** MALLOC FAILED ***` or hung with no message. Each task stack has a 256-byte MPU guard at its low end, moved on every context switch; `NROS_FREERTOS_TASK_STACK_GUARD=0` builds without it.
