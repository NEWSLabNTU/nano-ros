The hosted C++ surface now takes the rclcpp a stock ROS 2 package writes (phase-488, found porting Autoware's `autoware_external_cmd_selector`). A service handler can carry state: `create_service(name, std::bind(&X::on_request, this, _1, _2))` and capturing lambdas are accepted in every handler shape, their capture is held by the service's executor entry, and a `bool` return is ignored as upstream ignores it. `create_service` takes upstream's trailing QoS and callback group, with the `rmw_qos_profile_*` constants. `create_callback_group(rclcpp::CallbackGroupType::...)`, `CallbackGroup::SharedPtr`, `SubscriptionOptions::callback_group` and the four-argument `create_subscription(topic, qos, callback, options)` exist; an unnamed group runs on the node's default context. On the std surface `publish()` may be called as a statement under `-Werror`, and a failed publish is logged. `diagnostic_updater::Updater` publishes on its own timer as upstream's does, can be built from `this`, and `<diagnostic_updater/update_functions.hpp>` exists (without its classes, issue 2040). A node of an ament-built package serves its parameters with no extra call when its runtime carries `param_services`. The "executor full" log names `NROS_EXECUTOR_MAX_CBS`.
