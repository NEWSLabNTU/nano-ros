Opening an executor costs the caller's stack less. `Executor::open` and `Executor::open_in` used to inline a session, a table layout, log buffers and executor copies into the frame that opened them, about 5.9 KB on a Cortex-M3 FreeRTOS task. The open now runs in one bounded out-of-line frame and returns the executor straight into the caller's result, 1.2 to 2.1 KB shallower along the open path. A binary no longer needs its own `#[inline(never)]` wrapper to keep its run closure small.
