A C/C++ native level with a Linux `SCHED_DEADLINE` budget now keeps its reservation. The level thread asked the kernel for it while the spawner was still re-applying the level's `SCHED_FIFO` priority to the new thread; when the spawner's write landed second the reservation was gone, and because the executor had handed the budget to the kernel, nothing enforced it (5 of 62 runs with `CAP_SYS_NICE`). The level now waits for the spawn to return before it asks. The kernel-throttle tests also judge the mean wall time over releases, since a tick-enforced kernel (HZ=250, `HRTICK_DL` off) lets single releases overrun (issue 2066).
