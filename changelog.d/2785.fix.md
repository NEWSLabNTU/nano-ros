A Zephyr image whose socket service stack (`CONFIG_NET_SOCKETS_SERVICE_STACK_SIZE`) cannot hold the poll array Zephyr puts on it (one `k_poll_event` per `CONFIG_NET_SOCKETS_POLL_MAX` entry) is now refused at configure, naming both knobs and the least stack size. The overflow ran into .bss with no message; on a Cyclone image with a large poll maximum it landed on the log dispatch guard and silenced every nano-ros log record while the image kept running.
