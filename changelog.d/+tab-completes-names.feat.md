`<Tab>` now completes the names `nros` verbs take: `nros board show <Tab>` lists board keys, `nros rmw show <Tab>` the RMWs, and `nros knob show <Tab>` the knobs. `nros completions` prints the line that turns it on for your shell; `--static` prints the old generated script, which completes verbs and flags only. Sourcing `activate.sh` or `activate.fish` turns it on in an interactive shell, and the installer and the first `nros setup` print the line.
