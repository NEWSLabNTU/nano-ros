A zenoh image that dials `can/...` or `isotp/...` on a platform whose zenoh-pico port has no arm for that link (`freertos-posix`, `threadx-linux`) is now refused by `nros configure`, naming the platform and the platforms that have one, instead of failing at the final link with `undefined reference to _z_open_can`. The zenoh port rows state the links they carry (`[port.<platform>] links`). A gate (`just check zenoh-port-links`) derives those lists from zenoh-pico's sources and fails when one drifts.
