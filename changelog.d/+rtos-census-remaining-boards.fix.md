The FreeRTOS POSIX simulator now uses 8 priorities, as the hardware boards do, and its timer task sits at 4. With 10 it ran zenoh-pico's read task at 5 and the timer task at 9, outside the FreeRTOS priority plan. The ThreadX plan's foreign band now holds the riscv64 board's NetX Duo threads at 1 and 2, and the NuttX plan's holds the rv-virt high-priority work queue at 224. Single-executor FreeRTOS and ThreadX images now print the boot task census too.
