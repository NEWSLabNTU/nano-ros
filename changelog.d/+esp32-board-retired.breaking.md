The ESP32-C3 bare-metal board (`nros-board-esp32-qemu`, `nros-platform-esp32-qemu`, `examples/esp32-c3-baremetal/`) is retired: it had no receive interrupt and no one-shot timer behind a wake object, so it could not keep the real-time wait promise (RFC-0113 D5). Every name it answered to (`esp32-c3-baremetal`, `esp32-qemu`, `esp32-c3`, `esp32c3`, `esp32`) and the `esp32` `nros::main!` framework are refused naming that reason. The `platform-esp32-qemu` features of `nros-platform`, `nros-c` and `nros-cpp` are removed.
