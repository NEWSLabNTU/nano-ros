A workspace that keeps its out-of-source build trees beside `src/` is no longer walked through them. The prereq scan's two `package.xml` walks pruned `build` and `target-*` but not `build-*`, so every `build-<name>` directory a `west build -d` or `cmake -B` had left behind was scanned in full: measured on a tree with 85 of them, one `nros image-facts` opened 362,782 directories, 98.7% of every open it made, to read a single file. Both walks now share one pruning predicate, which also honours the ament ignore markers the pkg-index walk already honoured.
