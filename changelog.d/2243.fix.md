The PX4 SITL test harness boots at most one PX4 daemon per host at a time. PX4 keys its server lock and client socket on the instance under `/tmp`, and the nightly `px4 sitl` job's two booting tests started two instance-0 daemons at once, so whichever lost the race timed out with an empty log. `Px4Sitl::boot_in` now holds a host-wide lock on the instance until its daemon is dead, and fails at once, by name, when a daemon it did not start already serves the instance.
