A spawned executor level now parks at the platform's finest park (microseconds on Linux) instead of whole milliseconds. A level's park is the time to its next timer, and rounding it up to the millisecond made a 1 kHz level whose callback took 300 us release every 1.3 ms and drop a period in four. This affects every C/C++ level runner and `nros::levels`; a platform with no microsecond park keeps the millisecond wait.
`rclc_executor_spin_some` and `rclcpp::Clock::sleep_until` / `sleep_for` also take their timeout to the microsecond now, instead of rounding it to whole milliseconds.
