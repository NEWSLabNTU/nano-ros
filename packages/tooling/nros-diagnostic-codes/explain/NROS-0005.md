A board names a platform that is not one of nano-ros's built-in platforms, and
the platform's `inherits` chain does not reach one, so nano-ros does not know
which family's build rules the platform takes.

A platform you add yourself (`nros new platform`) is described by its
`nros-platform.toml`. Its `inherits` key names the built-in family it builds
like (`freertos`, `zephyr`, `bare-metal`, and so on), directly or through
another platform that does. The refusal names the chain it followed, and says
which of these went wrong:

- the chain ends at a platform that inherits nothing;
- a name in the chain is neither built in nor answered by any
  `nros-platform.toml` on the platform search path;
- the chain loops back on itself.

## How to fix it

Add `inherits` to the platform the refusal names, choosing the built-in family
it is closest to:

```toml
# nros-platform.toml
inherits = "freertos"
```

A platform that does not exist yet is scaffolded with
`nros new platform <name> --inherits <family>`.
